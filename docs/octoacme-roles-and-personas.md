# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Security / Compliance Lead

### Role Summary
The Security / Compliance Lead ensures that delivery work meets required security, privacy, and compliance standards while balancing business priorities and delivery speed.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture and features for security and privacy risks
- Own compliance requirements, controls, and audit readiness for the project
- Partner with engineering to define secure design patterns and mitigation plans
- Coordinate vulnerability triage, remediation tracking, and escalation when risks exceed tolerance
- Support stakeholder communication on security requirements and exceptions

### Goals
- Reduce product and process risk
- Ensure compliance obligations are met without delaying core delivery
- Build trust with customers, stakeholders, and regulators

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and architecture checkpoints
- Risk and mitigation updates in project meetings
- Compliance review and exception documentation for leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to ensure requirements account for security and compliance constraints
- Partners with Project Managers on risk tracking, escalation, and dependency management
- Collaborates with Developers on secure implementation and remediation planning
- Supports QA Lead in validating security-related acceptance criteria and release readiness

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
The QA Lead defines quality standards, validation strategy, and release gates so that delivery teams consistently meet acceptance criteria and customer needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage expectations, and quality milestones
- Review requirements and acceptance criteria for testability and completeness
- Coordinate manual and automated testing across functional and non-functional scenarios
- Identify quality risks, defects, and release blockers early in the lifecycle
- Partner with release and engineering teams to confirm readiness before deployment

### Goals
- Improve confidence in releases and reduce production issues
- Align quality checks with business impact and user experience
- Maintain clear quality ownership across project phases

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, validation reports, and defect triage
- Release readiness reviews and QA checkpoints
- Cross-functional quality updates in sprint and milestone meetings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to translate requirements into measurable acceptance criteria
- Collaborates with Developers on defect prioritization, testability, and automation opportunities
- Coordinates with Project Managers to track quality risks and dependencies
- Provides evidence to Security / Compliance Lead and Release Manager for release validation and compliance checks

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
The Release Manager coordinates release plans, quality gates, and rollout communications so that features reach users with minimal disruption and clear accountability.

### Responsibilities
- Plan deployment windows, rollback strategy, and release sequencing
- Coordinate pre-release readiness reviews and post-release verification
- Maintain release notes, operational checklists, and stakeholder communications
- Align engineering, QA, and project stakeholders around launch timing and risk
- Escalate issues when release criteria or readiness thresholds are not met

### Goals
- Improve release predictability and reduce operational surprises
- Ensure changes are validated before and after deployment
- Maintain a clear path for rollback and incident handling

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and deployment plans
- Stakeholder launch updates and rollback status reports
- Post-release review and issue escalation communications

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Product Managers on launch priorities and communications
- Coordinates with Project Managers on milestones, dependencies, and risk mitigation
- Works with Developers and QA Lead to confirm release readiness and validation evidence
- Coordinates with Security / Compliance Lead on any security or compliance controls required during rollout

---

## Engineering Manager / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
The Engineering Manager or Technical Lead ensures that technical execution, team capacity, and delivery priorities stay aligned with project goals and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical direction, architecture decisions, and implementation standards
- Manage delivery capacity, work allocation, and team dependencies
- Remove blockers, support technical trade-offs, and align engineering decisions with product priorities
- Help identify delivery risks early and coordinate mitigation across teams
- Support performance, maintainability, and operational readiness of the solution

### Goals
- Deliver sustainable engineering execution
- Improve predictability and technical quality across the project lifecycle
- Keep delivery plans realistic and aligned to team capacity

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning meetings and dependency reviews
- Technical trade-off discussions and design decisions
- Status updates on delivery health, delivery risks, and escalations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to translate strategy into feasible delivery plans
- Works closely with Project Managers on schedule, scope, and dependency tracking
- Supports Developers with technical decisions and execution planning
- Coordinates with QA Lead and Release Manager to validate quality and readiness across stages of delivery

---

## Business Sponsor / Stakeholder Champion

### Role Summary
The Business Sponsor or Stakeholder Champion represents the business case for the project, provides strategic direction, and helps resolve cross-functional decisions that require executive support.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business objectives, priorities, and expected outcomes
- Sponsor funding, staffing, and strategic trade-offs when needed
- Provide executive-level decision support and issue escalation
- Help align stakeholders around scope, timeline, and value realization
- Champion adoption and accountability for business outcomes after delivery

### Goals
- Ensure the project stays connected to business value and stakeholder needs
- Support timely decisions and escalation when dependencies or risks emerge
- Maximize project impact and adoption

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee or stakeholder updates
- Business case review, milestone approvals, and priority decisions
- Issue escalation and dependency resolution with leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to prioritize outcomes and validate success metrics
- Provides direction to Project Managers on strategic trade-offs and escalations
- Partner with Engineering Manager / Technical Lead and Security / Compliance Lead to understand operational and risk implications
- Supports Release Manager and QA Lead by reinforcing readiness expectations and launch communication needs

---
